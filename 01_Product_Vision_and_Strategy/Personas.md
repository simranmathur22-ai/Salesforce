# Personas

## Persona A – Klaus, the Dealership Service Manager (external)
- **Age / context:** 47, runs the service desk of a mid-sized car dealership in Stuttgart; 30–40 warranty cases a month
- **Tech comfort:** Medium. Uses e-mail and the dealer management system daily; wants no training
- **Goals:** Submit a claim in under 5 minutes; know the status without calling; get paid faster
- **Pain points:** Re-sending PDFs, no confirmation, claims "disappearing"
- **Needs from the product:** Simple guided form, photo upload from phone, own-claims list, e-mail confirmations
- **Quote:** *"I just want to know whether my claim is approved – without phoning anybody."*

## Persona B – Julia, the Internal Warranty Agent (internal)
- **Age / context:** 34, warranty team at the manufacturer, 60+ claims a week
- **Tech comfort:** High. Works in Salesforce all day
- **Goals:** Spend time on complex claims, not trivial ones; decide with full evidence at hand
- **Pain points:** Copy-pasting from PDFs, missing photos, unclear priority, GDPR audits
- **Needs from the product:** Queue of claims needing review, all claim data and evidence on one page, status change in one click, confidence that PII is handled
- **Quote:** *"Don't send me a €80 claim – send me the ones that need my judgement."*
